LoRaDev

App v3 · Bluetooth · LoRa 433.175 MHz

Offline
Send text messages through the LoRa network.
  1. Power the LoRaDev board and keep the phone close (Bluetooth range is short on this board).
  2. Tap Connect and choose LoRaDev (it may be listed as P2PSRV1).
  3. Write up to 42 characters and tap Send. Replies from ChirpStack appear here.
